# DDInter 2.0 data attribution

Anuvaad includes the eight downloadable drug-pair CSV files published by the DDInter 2.0 team: ATC groups A, B, D, H, L, P, R, and V. The files were obtained from the [official DDInter 2.0 download page](https://ddinter2.scbdd.com/download/) on 2026-10-06. The source page describes a current release updated 2024-05-14.

**Attribution:** Tian Y, Yi J, Wang N, Wu C, Peng J, Liu S, Yang G, Cao D. “DDInter 2.0: an enhanced drug interaction resource with expanded data coverage, new interaction types, and improved user interface.” *Nucleic Acids Research*. 2025;53(D1):D1356–D1362. [doi:10.1093/nar/gkae726](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkae726). Database: [DDInter 2.0](https://ddinter2.scbdd.com/).

The eight CSV files contain 222,383 source rows before deduplication. They cover only the eight downloadable ATC groups, not all DDInter drug pairs. Their exported fields provide drug names and a Minor/Moderate/Major level; they do not provide a mechanism or patient-specific assessment for each row. DDInter warns that the database can be incomplete or contain errors and that an absent pair is not evidence of safety. An exact name match in Anuvaad is a source-listed interaction review finding, not a complete clinical judgment.
