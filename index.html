Architecture Overview

Hybrid Engine Pipeline

Connected local services
1

Configured safety rules

Prescription/record OCR and scispaCy BC5CDR biomedical NER propose literal chemical and disease mentions for human review. The model does not identify active status, allergy, or a confirmed diagnosis.

2

PostgreSQL + Neo4j context graph

PostgreSQL stores profiles and scans; Neo4j stores configured rule context. FHIR R4 Bundle import/export and SMART authorization are available when an EHR administrator registers the app.

3

Machine learning validation status

No patient-level ML risk predictor is enabled. No clinically sound labeled outcome dataset is available; a speculative probability could create false reassurance. The deterministic rules and reference evidence remain the safety findings.

4

Explanation and voice output

Safety findings are spoken in English, Hindi, or Telugu. With consent, Gemini can rewrite selected findings in plain language; it cannot alter the safety analysis.
